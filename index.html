Why this exists

I got tired of seeing the same number.

For nearly a year, the scale kept circling the 190s. High protein alone did not fix it. Starving until dinner made the evening worse. So the goal changed: build a system that is filling, repeatable, measurable and strong enough for real life.

“Build the habit. Uncover the body. Document the journey.”

The KaluzyFit method

Five parts. One feedback loop.

01

Soup-centered meals

Vegetables for volume, protein for muscle, herbs and acid for flavor, portions for progress.

02

Home strength

A bench, yoga mat and two 25-pound dumbbells are enough to build momentum.

03

Daily movement

Walking supports the calorie deficit, digestion, mood and consistency.

04

Recovery and mindset

Sleep, hydration, minimum-day workouts and planned enjoyment keep one hard day from becoming a lost week.

05

Track and adjust

Use weekly averages, waist measurements, steps, sleep and adherence. Change one variable at a time.

01 · TRANSFORMATION

Uncover the body already there.

Lift four days, walk most days, keep protein steady, reduce the calories that sneak in, and judge the seven-day trend rather than one morning.

02 · SOUP METHOD

Volume from plants. Strength from protein.

Build the bowl with vegetables, lentils or beans, then add a measured portion of chicken, fish, tofu, eggs, or yogurt.

Base onion · garlic · gingerSpice cumin · turmeric · paprikaFinish lemon · herbs · yogurt

03 · HOME WORKOUT

Master the routine before upgrading the room.

Your bench, yoga mat, and two 25-pound dumbbells are enough for a real three-day strength plan.

Mon Chest + shouldersWed Core + full bodyFri Upper bodySat Steps + mobility

04 · MINDSET

A reduced day still protects the habit.

Green day: full workout. Yellow day: the first three exercises. Red day: five minutes, one movement, and a walk.

Never miss twice.

05 · SLEEP + RECOVERY

Protect tomorrow with tonight.

Aim for 7–8 hours. Finish the largest meal 2–3 hours before bed, cut caffeine early, and shorten long naps.

7–8 hrPhone downShort napRecover

06 · HYDRATION + ENJOYMENT

Tea supports the plan. It does not replace it.

Use green or black tea earlier, ginger after meals, and chamomile in the evening. Enjoy one planned meal, not a runaway cheat day.

Morning green or blackAfter meal gingerEvening chamomile

07 · TRACK + ADJUST

Data turns frustration into a decision.

Log morning weight, waist, steps, workouts, sleep, adherence, digestion, and useful notes. Review the seven-day average every week.

Why this exists

I got tired of seeing the same number.

For nearly a year, the scale kept circling the 190s. High protein alone did not fix it. Starving until dinner made the evening worse. So the goal changed: build a system that is filling, repeatable, measurable and strong enough for real life.

“Build the habit. Uncover the body. Document the journey.”

The KaluzyFit method

Five parts. One feedback loop.

01

Soup-centered meals

Vegetables for volume, protein for muscle, herbs and acid for flavor, portions for progress.

02

Home strength

A bench, yoga mat and two 25-pound dumbbells are enough to build momentum.

03

Daily movement

Walking supports the calorie deficit, digestion, mood and consistency.

04

Recovery and mindset

Sleep, hydration, minimum-day workouts and planned enjoyment keep one hard day from becoming a lost week.

05

Track and adjust

Use weekly averages, waist measurements, steps, sleep and adherence. Change one variable at a time.

01

Transformation vision

Lose fat while keeping muscle. First reach 187, then 185, then reassess. Lighting and posture help the picture, but consistency creates the body.

Lift 4× · Walk daily · Protein each meal · Moderate deficit
02

The soup method

Build a large, satisfying bowl from vegetables, lentils or beans, a measured protein and a low-sodium flavor base.

03

Home workout momentum

Master the routine at home before upgrading equipment. Use the bench, yoga mat and two 25-pound dumbbells.

04

Minimum-day mindset

Green day: full workout. Yellow day: first three exercises. Red day: five minutes, one exercise and a walk. Never miss twice.

A reduced session still protects the identity.
05

Sleep and recovery

Aim for 7–8 hours, finish the large meal 2–3 hours before bed, cut caffeine early and keep naps short enough to protect nighttime sleep.

Protect tomorrow with tonight’s routine.
06

Hydration and enjoyment

Tea supports the routine but is not magic. Choose one planned enjoyment meal, not an uncontrolled cheat day, then return at the next meal.
